Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Buildhaus ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What information we collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, company name (if provided), and the details of your enquiry. We do not collect any payment information through this website.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry and communicate with you about your project. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

How we store your information

Enquiries submitted through our contact form are processed by Formspree and delivered to our email. We retain email correspondence for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected. You can request deletion at any time.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies. If we add analytics in future, this policy will be updated and a cookie notice will be displayed.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, email us at buildhausdesign@gmail.com.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at buildhausdesign@gmail.com.
